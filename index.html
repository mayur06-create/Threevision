COMPLIANCE & PERMITS

Government Plan Sanction Process

We navigate the complex municipal guidelines and regulations to secure your legal building clearances.

Hassle-Free Legal Clearance

Securing local municipal approval is critical before breaking ground. Building laws involve complex calculations regarding Floor Area Ratio (FAR), setbacks, height constraints, parking allotments, and fire code requirements.

We handle the complete plan sanction process on your behalf

Our engineers compile necessary clearance sets, coordinate directly with municipal inspectors, and verify all design compliance requirements to secure your construction permit smoothly.

Submit Document Checklist
1

Document Collection

We assemble property deed papers, land surveys, taxation documents, and owner identifications.

2

Drawing Submission

We create specialized municipal drawings conforming precisely to building bylaws and submit online files.

3

Authority Approval

We arrange coordinate site checks, address administrative clarifications, and resolve code questions.

4

Final Sanction

You receive the official stamped building sanction certificate and license permit to kick off your building process.
